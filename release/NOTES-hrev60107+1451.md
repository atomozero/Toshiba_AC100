RenkuOS, a fork of Haiku, for the Toshiba AC100 (Tegra 2, 512 MB RAM), on a microSD card. Nothing is written to the internal eMMC: switch the AC100 off and Android is still there.

### New since hrev60107+1273
- **Install on an 8 GB eMMC.** Some AC100s have an 8 GB internal memory instead of 16 GB: "Install to internal memory" refused them ("no 16 GB eMMC found"). It now takes both, and checks first that what is on the microSD card fits in the internal memory.
- **USB microphones as a normal audio input.** The microphone of a USB webcam is now handled only by the system's USB audio driver: it appears in Preferences > Media as an input with the device's name (for example "AUKEY PC-LM1E Camera"), and can be chosen there as the input for every application. Its volume works: some microphones give a range smaller than a decibel, which was read as a single step. BubiCam 2.1.2 records the camera's sound from that input; the webcam driver (aukey_webcam_v4 0.9.1) only handles the picture now.
- **RenkuOS updated** to its current main branch (176 commits since +1273).
- **Cinema** 1.6.5 and **Atomo123** 0.9.1 (its start screen closes on time on the AC100).

### Fixed in +1273 and +1272
- **USB microphones and sound cards.** A fix of the USB driver that went into +1272 made every other transfer of a USB sound device come back empty; fixed in +1273.
- **Terminal works.** In the +736 image bash could not start (`libreadline.so` missing), so the Terminal and every shell script failed. The image script did not add what the full bash and coreutils need: readline, gettext, libiconv and libunistring are now in, and every package of the image was checked to find what it needs.

### New in +1272 and +1273
- **Webcams.** USB (UVC) webcams work through a new driver (aukey_webcam_v4) and the BubiCam app; their microphone through the system's USB audio driver. Video and sound are read through isochronous streams that the system keeps queued (taken from air/OS), and three fixes to the EHCI USB driver remove the gaps and stale data that broke frames. Tested with an AUKEY PC-LM1E: 320x240 is clean with the microphone on; at 640x480 and above our camera still loses part of its frames.
- **Cinema**: videos and live TV channels. For YouTube it needs `yt-dlp`, which is not in the image: put the `yt-dlp` file from its GitHub releases in `~/config/non-packaged/bin` and make it executable.
- **Lid.** Closing the lid switches the screen off and saves power; opening it brings the network back first.
- **Sound.** The audio codec is powered down while nothing plays; the microphone no longer wakes it while it sleeps.
- **Bluetooth.** Speakers paired after start-up are found, a connection is announced once it is secured, speakers that refuse are called back less and less often.
- **Reliability.** The microSD driver reads safely on the Tegra 2, the keyboard/touchpad controller is started over after a timeout instead of freezing the input, a Wi-Fi memory leak is gone, restarting the media services no longer crashes them, and a USB device unplugged in the middle of a transfer no longer leaves every other device waiting (an air/OS fix).
- ActivityMonitor shows the temperature; many smaller fixes, all of them in `patches/renkuos`.

### What works
Display (1024x600, native driver, HDMI mirror at 720p), keyboard and touchpad, battery, both CPU cores with frequency scaling, microSD and eMMC, USB, USB webcams, the internal Wi-Fi with WPA2, sound (speakers, headphones, built-in microphone, Bluetooth speakers), clock, power off, the lid. Bluetooth keyboards, mice and gamepads, tested with an Xbox Wireless Controller and the internal CSR adapter.

Applications: WebPositive, Cinema, BubiCam, LocalSend, Genio, BePDF, WonderBrush, Atomo123, R Television, Cricket, a SIP softphone, SalaGiochi (games for children with libretro cores; no games are included, "Add games" puts the ones you own in place), and git, curl, ssh, nano and other command line tools.

### Not yet
3D acceleration, suspend. Restarting needs U-Boot in the eMMC; with the USB boot below, switch off and start again instead. The L2 cache is off.

The Wi-Fi driver is for the module of our unit, a Ralink RT8070 (MSI MS-3871). If your AC100 has another one and the Wi-Fi does not show up, please open an issue with the output of `listusb` from the Terminal.

This image was built and checked like the previous ones but has not yet been started from a freshly written card; please report what you find.

### Install
You need a microSD card of 8 GB or more, a mini-USB cable and a PC with Linux. In short: write the image to the card (balenaEtcher and Rufus take the `.img.xz` as it is), put the AC100 in recovery mode (switched off, hold Ctrl+Esc and press the power button), read its boot configuration once with `tegrarcm --bct=ac100.bct readbct`, then start U-Boot with `tegrarcm --bct=ac100.bct --bootloader=u-boot-ac100.bin --loadaddr=0x108000`; U-Boot starts RenkuOS from the card. The full steps are in [INSTALL.md](https://github.com/atomozero/Toshiba_AC100/blob/main/INSTALL.md). Once it runs, [USING.md](https://github.com/atomozero/Toshiba_AC100/blob/main/USING.md) explains the keys, the battery and power settings, sound, the microphone and Bluetooth.

"Install to internal memory", on the desktop, copies the system to the eMMC and **replaces Android**; make a backup of the eMMC first, as INSTALL.md explains.

The SSH server does not start by itself. Check the files against the `.sha256` files.

### Sources
The system is RenkuOS `024d1e98dc` plus the patches in [`patches/renkuos`](https://github.com/atomozero/Toshiba_AC100/tree/main/patches/renkuos): one patch with the AC100 tree at the merge of RenkuOS main, then the commit after it (the full list of commits is in `COMMITS.txt`). The isochronous streams and the unplug fix of `usb_raw` come from air/OS (https://github.com/airos-works/haiku, MIT) and keep their author. U-Boot is upstream `8d534cec` plus the patches and the configuration in [`u-boot`](https://github.com/atomozero/Toshiba_AC100/tree/main/u-boot) (GPL-2.0+). The README explains how to build both. The webcam driver is https://github.com/atomozero/haiku-uvc-webcam (0.9.1 is commit `1450201` of its `fase0-lifecycle` branch), Cinema is https://github.com/atomozero/Cinema.
